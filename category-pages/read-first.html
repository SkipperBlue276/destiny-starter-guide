↪ What Even is D2?

Hello!

If you're here, it's probably already too late.

I played very little of Destiny 1, catching only the tail end around Rise of Iron, and I certainly never did any of the higher tier content. I remember launching into Crota's End with two of my other (like me, barely teenage) friends, and not even getting to the first lantern in the first encounter, because we didn't even understand what a 'raid' or 'encounter' was.

I pre-ordered Destiny 2 for the Xbox One once I heard it was coming out, but was never able to actually play, because my shitty country internet perpetually Weaseled me; I never was able to even finish the Red War campaign. I only really picked it back up and started to "get it" around 2022, when I reinstalled it on my PC during Season of Plunder to see what the Weasel had stolen from my teenage self, and I've been playing semi-consistently since.

With the game now 'over,' I've, ironically, been playing a lot recently, and frankly think that it's in the best state it's been in for quite a while. Given this and the summer sale putting the full expansion collection on sale, there's somehow new people getting into the game, likely due to people like me who've been pestering their friends to play for years.

These guides are for those people, who aren't familiar with Destiny 2 at all, and are rightfully confused about the 5 year backlog of campaigns, dungeons, meta shifts, and other assorted content that's accumulated since Witch Queen in 2022.

These guides will seek to explain two main things:

1. The basic navigation, goals, activities, history, and tools available in Destiny 2 as of Moments of Triumph and the final update June 9, 2026; the 'large scale' stuff that isn't literally playing and shooting enemies.

2. The fundamentals on how abilities, combat, and buildcrafting work. This guide will not cover things like the current meta, or what specific builds are 'optimal.' Instead, the goal of these is to explain how to create a build, or understand how a build works; not what's 'optimal,' but how to optimize.

Don't be too concerned if you don't pick everything up instantly; if you need to come back to reference, this will be here until Github terminates my account. This is mostly just to get you familiar with the verbiage surrounding the game, and to centralize actually up-to-date and relevant information about it, given so much of the information available as of now is completely irrelevant at best, and straight up wrong at worst.

All that being said, what the hell even is Destiny 2?
